# Social Media Operating Directive — 2026-09-29

Founder directive: every entity POC is responsible for keeping that entity's social account alive daily. Higher posting volume is intentional. Post, measure, keep winners, and archive weak/off-brand posts later instead of default deletion.

## Universal POC rules
1. Check the entity account morning, afternoon, and evening.
2. Verify scheduled posts actually published. A provider post ID + permalink is the publication truth.
3. Add real-time content from the entity; do not wait for perfect creative.
4. Reply to comments and engage the target audience daily.
5. Repost/tag relevant customers, partners, venues, creators and collaborators.
6. If a graphic/video/caption is missing, request it immediately. No silent page.
7. Preserve each brand identity. Never merge brands.
8. Archive weak/off-brand content later; do not delete by default.
