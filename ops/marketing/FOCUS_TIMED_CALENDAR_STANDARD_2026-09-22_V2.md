## Caption rule
Original short hook -> useful truthful detail -> genuinely involved account tags -> one action -> relevant hashtags.

The general voice is confident, witty, culture-aware and concise, not copied lyrics or a claim that a named artist wrote the copy. STUSH has its own sharper fashion voice. S.O.S. keeps provider and safety language clear. Sole Exchange keeps recipients' dignity. Beverage copy must not invent product benefits. A business-to-business email remains useful and professional even when social copy has attitude.

## Mention and hashtag rule
Tag every relevant participant whose role and current account are verified: featured venue, promoter, organizer, artist, host, photographer, model, stylist or creator. Use caption mentions, appropriate media credits and Story mentions where useful. A tag is not an accepted collaboration invitation, endorsement or permission to reuse content. Never invent a handle or add unrelated famous accounts to chase reach. When a handle is unverified, keep that final check visible and use the verified name until resolved.

Use 3–5 relevant brand/city/topic hashtags in feed copy as the editorial default. Do not describe them as guaranteed reach or add irrelevant trending tags. Stories prioritize clear text, useful stickers and an actual link or relevant reply prompt.

## Simple operator screen
Open your company -> pick today's date -> open the next time card -> open the media and source links -> copy the caption -> check the real accounts, facts and destination -> obtain exact approval -> submit once through the authorized route -> check the published result.

Do not call a planning time a provider schedule. Do not publish unapproved creative to hit a volume target. Replace a missing time-sensitive feature with a prepared relevant evergreen feature, or explicitly move the slot. Do not backdate or flood the page with missed posts.
