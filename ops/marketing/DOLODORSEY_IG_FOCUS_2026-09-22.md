## Creative rule: never lead with promo
A naked flyer, product grid, discount card or obvious advertisement should not be the first frame of a normal @DOLODORSEY feed post.

Preferred wrappers:
- **Founder-first carousel:** founder/lifestyle image → BTS/context → brand/event asset → flyer/CTA.
- **Lifestyle reel:** founder/scene hook → process/BTS → brand reveal → brief flyer/product reveal at the end.
- **Flyer-in-world:** flyer appears on a wall, screen, poster, phone, laptop, billboard or other environmental element inside a lifestyle visual.
- **Story sequence:** lifestyle/context Story first; direct flyer or CTA can follow.
- **BTS proof:** meetings, samples, studio, fittings, packaging, operations and build process before the polished brand asset.
