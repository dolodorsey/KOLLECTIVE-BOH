1. Exact entity/location identity.
2. Exact entity credential.
3. Exact sender identity.
4. Suppression/eligibility enforcement.
5. Provider acceptance.
6. Delivery/inbox evidence.
7. Receipt stored against the same entity.

**Do not report an entity ready based on another entity’s evidence.**
