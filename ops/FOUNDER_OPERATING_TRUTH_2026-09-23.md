## HighLevel / outbound

- **GHL is primary** for marketing email, newsletters, nurture, provider recruitment, customer lifecycle, operational follow-up and SMS.

- Gmail is exception-only for VC/investor outreach, founder-personal correspondence or another explicitly approved special case.

- Website changes require production deployment plus live verification.
- Each company is certified independently.
