## Task granularity rule
This program is represented by **one enterprise task/program card**.

Do **not** create separate tasks for:
- GOOD TIMES capital outreach
- S.O.S. capital outreach
- MISSION 365 capital outreach
- NTF capital outreach
- BEVCO capital outreach
- individual investors or firms
- individual contacts
- individual emails
- follow-ups
- replies
- meeting requests

Those are **lanes and pipeline records**, not tasks.

A new task is appropriate only when there is a distinct finite deliverable that needs independent ownership, approval, or completion proof, such as a legal filing, a specific data-room package, or a founder-approved financing document.

## First-touch rule
First touch is minimum disclosure:
- founder identity and credible operating context
- high-level category
- verified reason the investor may fit
- request for a 15–20 minute founder call

Do not send confidential mechanics, valuation, detailed terms, trade secrets, unverified metrics, or a broad deck before the appropriate stage.

## Enterprise task surface

**Enterprise Capital & Investor Acquisition**
